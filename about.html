About Me

Faruk Pasic is a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Institute of Telecommunications, TU Wien, working in the field of wireless communications and multi-band RF systems.

He received the B.Sc. degree in electrical engineering from the University of Sarajevo, Bosnia and Herzegovina, in 2017, the Dipl.-Ing. degree (M.Sc. equivalent) in telecommunications engineering from TU Wien, Austria, in 2021, and the Dr. Techn. degree (Ph.D. equivalent) in telecommunications engineering from TU Wien in 2026.

He joined the Institute of Telecommunications, TU Wien, in 2019. His research focuses on wireless channel measurements and modeling, channel estimation, millimeter-wave (mmWave) communications, MIMO systems, multi-band RF transceiver systems, machine-learning-based channel modeling, and out-of-band aided communication.
